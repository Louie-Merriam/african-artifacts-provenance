## Accuracy flags before submission

1. **Burton quote** — "well-directed blows in the spot where they are most likely to take effect": the scholarly treatment of Burton's ethnography is Maxwell 2008; verify the exact wording and page there (or in Burton's own writings) before attributing.
2. **Guy Burrows was British, not Belgian** — a British officer *in the service of* the Congo Free State. Suggest: "Captain Guy Burrows, a British officer serving in Leopold's Congo Free State."
3. **"average date of 2006"** — it's the **median** acquisition year, and the UNESCO-1970 causal reading is an interpretation: the fair cluster is dominated by contemporary purchases and donations, which post-date 1970 for many reasons. Suggest "median" + "consistent with, though not attributable solely to, the post-1970 norm shift (UNESCO 1970)."
4. **African Arts author name** — the 2025 Ghana/Wellcome article's author should be checked against the journal page (direct.mit.edu/afar 58(1), p.7) before final formatting; the article itself is verified.
5. Digital Benin count: the registry pull was 954 entries; your 951 (784 + 167) is correct after excluding three non-actor entries — consider stating "954 registry entries, of which 951 usable."
